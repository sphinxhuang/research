02 · Physical sequence

One signal, eight commercial touchpoints

The photon is born only after the GPU creates an electrical bitstream. At long distances the link changes from short-reach direct detect to coherent transport.

  1. Accelerator demand originNVIDIA, AMD, and custom accelerators emit electrical SerDes lanes and create the bandwidth requirement.
  2. Switch ASIC + lanesArista, Cisco, NVIDIA, Broadcom, and Marvell steer traffic into high-speed electrical lanes.
  3. DSP, driver, modulatorMACOM, Coherent, Lumentum, Marvell, and Broadcom condition and modulate the signal.
  4. Laser + optical engineCoherent and Lumentum supply EML, CW, VCSEL, SiPh, and packaged-engine options.
  5. Fiber + connector pathCorning, SENKO, US Conec, and AFL/Fujikura carry and organize the photons.
  6. Photodiode, TIA, DSPMACOM, Coherent, Lumentum, and Marvell turn light back into recoverable electrical data.
  7. Scale-across transportCiena, Nokia, and Cisco use coherent DSPs and line systems when distance demands it.
  8. Validation around the pathKeysight, VIAVI, and Anritsu test components, modules, fabrics, production, and field links.

03 · Layer index

What is bought, and by whom

Context vendors make the competitive map legible without turning every peer into an interactive node. The lists are representative, not exhaustive rankings.

Demand origin · not a constellation layer

Accelerators and platform owners

GPU and custom-accelerator platforms determine lane speed, topology, reach, power budget, and the choice between copper, pluggable optics, NPO, and CPO.

Representative: NVIDIA, AMD, hyperscaler custom accelerators.

Layer 1

Wafer fabrication + advanced packaging

Deposition, etch, lithography, inspection, epitaxy, hybrid bonding, and packaging turn optical and electronic designs into manufacturable devices.

Required: Applied Materials. Context: KLA, Lam Research, ASML.

Layer 2

Photonic + electronic components

EMLs, CW lasers, VCSELs, silicon photonics, modulators, drivers, TIAs, photodiodes, and DSPs create and recover the optical signal.

Required: Coherent, Lumentum, MACOM. Context: Broadcom, Marvell, Semtech, Tower Semiconductor.

Layer 3

Transceivers, engines + manufacturing

400G, 800G, and 1.6T pluggables or optical engines package photonics, electronics, thermal design, connectors, firmware, and test into a usable link.

Required: Coherent, Lumentum. Context: InnoLight, Eoptolink, Accelink, Fabrinet.

Layer 4

Fiber, connectors + cable plant

Single-mode and multicore fiber, ribbon cable, high-density connectors, shuffles, patching, and structured cabling form the physical optical path.

Required: Corning. Context: SENKO, US Conec, AFL/Fujikura.

Layer 5

Datacenter switching + routing

Switch ASICs, SerDes, systems, front-panel optics, congestion control, telemetry, and operating software keep GPUs fed with data.

Required: Arista, Cisco. Context: NVIDIA networking, Broadcom, Marvell, HPE/Juniper.

Layer 6

Coherent DCI + optical transport

Coherent DSPs, transponders, ROADMs, amplifiers, line systems, and IP-over-DWDM move high-capacity wavelengths across campuses and regions.

Required: Ciena, Nokia, Cisco. Context: Infinera heritage inside Nokia; HPE/Juniper routing.

Layer 7

Design, manufacturing + field test

BERTs, sampling and real-time oscilloscopes, TDECQ, traffic emulation, optical measurement, assurance, and field tools validate the entire lifecycle.

Required: Keysight, VIAVI. Context: Anritsu.

06 · Investor synthesis

The same AI build creates different earnings shapes

This is analytical mapping, not a valuation model, price target, or personalized recommendation.

Directness

ReportedCOHR, LITE, MTSI, GLW, ANET, CSCO, CIEN, and NOK ship into the deployed path. AMAT, KEYS, and VIAV enable it.

AnalysisDirectness improves unit-volume sensitivity, not automatically P&L quality. A direct component vendor can carry more yield, pricing, and customer risk than an enabling tool vendor.

Cyclicality

ReportedSystems and transport vendors cite large cloud/carrier customers; component vendors are building capacity; test vendors respond earlier in design and qualification.

AnalysisThe cycle propagates in sequence: lab/tool orders → component capacity → module ramp → switches/cable plant → coherent build. Inventory corrections can reverse that sequence unevenly.

Where margin can live

ReportedANET and CSCO combine hardware with software/services; CIEN/NOK add software and support; KEYS/VIAV sell specialized instruments and workflow software.

AnalysisDurable economics tend to sit where IP, qualification, operations, and switching/transport control are hardest to substitute. Module assembly alone is more exposed to price compression unless paired with scarce lasers, DSP, packaging, or scale.

Major risks

ReportedCustomer concentration is explicit at AMAT, COHR/LITE, ANET, and CIEN; Nokia and HPE are integrating acquired network portfolios; several 2026 roadmaps still target later availability.

AnalysisThe common failure modes are architecture substitution, delayed 1.6T/CPO ramps, yield/capacity imbalance, hyperscaler insourcing, merchant-silicon dependence, and confusing announced products with revenue-producing deployments.
